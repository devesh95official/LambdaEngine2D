# Tetris

10x20 Tetris with SRS rotation and wall kicks, hold, four next pieces, a 7-bag and a new level
every 10 lines.

Controls: Left/Right or A/D move, Up/X/W rotate, Z or Left Ctrl rotate back, Down/S soft drop,
Space hard drop, C or Left Shift hold, P or Esc pause, Enter or a click starts.

```sh
build/release/examples/arcade/02_tetris/tetris  # --bot to watch, --headless --bot --frames 3000 for a summary
```
