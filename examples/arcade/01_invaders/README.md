# Invaders

Space Invaders: a 5x11 formation that speeds up as it thins out, four shields that crumble, a
bonus UFO and three lives.

Controls: Left/Right or A/D move, Space, Up, W or a left click fire, Enter or Space start,
P or Esc pause.

```sh
build/release/examples/arcade/01_invaders/invaders  # --bot to watch, --headless --bot --frames 3000 for a summary
```
