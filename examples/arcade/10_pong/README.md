# Pong

Two paddles and a ball that speeds up on every hit. First to 7 wins, against the CPU or a friend.

Controls: W/S, Up/Down or the mouse move your paddle. In 2-player mode the right paddle uses
Up/Down. On the title, Tab switches 1P/2P and Space or a click starts. After a match, Space
plays again and T goes back to the title.

```sh
build/release/examples/arcade/10_pong/pong  # --bot to watch, --headless --bot --frames 3000 for a summary
```
