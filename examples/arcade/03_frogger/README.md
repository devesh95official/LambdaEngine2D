# Frogger

Get across five lanes of traffic and five of river into the homes at the top. 30 seconds per
frog, three lives, and the lanes get faster each level.

Controls: arrows/WASD hop, a left click hops toward the cursor, Space, Enter or a click start
and restart.

```sh
build/release/examples/arcade/03_frogger/frogger  # --bot to watch, --headless --bot --frames 3000 for a summary
```
