# Minesweeper

Beginner (9x9, 10 mines) or Intermediate (16x16, 40 mines). The first click is always safe.

Controls: left click or Space/Z reveals, right click or F/X flags, middle click, C or a left
click on a number chords. Arrows/WASD move the cursor. Pick a field on the title with 1/2 or a
click. R retries the same field, Esc goes back to the title.

```sh
build/release/examples/arcade/05_minesweeper/minesweeper  # --bot to watch, --headless --bot --frames 3000 for a summary
```
